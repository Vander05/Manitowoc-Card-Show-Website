What's on the tables

Every table is different — that's the point. Here's what to expect walking the ballroom floor.

01

Sports cards

Baseball, football, basketball, and hockey — from wax-pack vintage to this year's rookies.

02

Pokémon & TCG

Mostly Pokémon singles, booster boxes, and sealed product, plus some Magic: The Gathering and One Piece from local dealers.

03

Vintage & memorabilia

Autographs, game-used pieces, and vintage cardboard for the set-builders and history buffs.

04

50 dealer tables

A full ballroom of tables split between sports card and TCG dealers — one stop for both hobbies.

FAQ

Is this show for TCG, sports cards, or both?

Both. You'll find sports card dealers set up right alongside TCG dealers — mostly Pokémon, plus some Magic: The Gathering and One Piece — across all 50 tables, so one trip covers both hobbies.

Can I sell or trade cards I bring from home?

Bringing cards to trade with other attendees is welcome. If you want to set up as a seller with a table, see the Reserve a table section above.

Is there an ATM on site?
